About

I study how particle-scale geometry, interactions, and degradation shape the mechanical behavior of granular materials.

I am a Technical Chief in the Advanced Analysis Department, Civil Engineering Design Division at Kajima Corporation, Japan. I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from the University of Tsukuba in March 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Takashi Matsushima.

My research combines the level set discrete element method (LS-DEM), image-based particle reconstruction, and micro–meso structural analysis to connect particle-scale mechanisms with macroscopic behavior. My work spans particle morphology, abrasion and fragmentation, granular rheology, and segregation.

In engineering applications, I develop coupled numerical models for reinforced concrete, bentonite pellet filling, fresh concrete flow, and shield tunneling.
