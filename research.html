Research

Connecting realistic particles, granular structures, and engineering-scale behavior through computational mechanics.

01

Particle morphology & degradation

Developing LS-DEM frameworks to represent realistic particle shapes and model abrasion and fragmentation. Image-based reconstruction and spherical harmonics support investigations of how shape and surface roughness influence granular behavior.

02

Micro–meso structures & mechanics

Quantifying cells and orientationally ordered clusters in granular systems. By combining DEM simulations with statistical mechanics, this work examines the structural origins of strength and deformation.

03

Granular flow & segregation

Investigating the rheology of bidisperse granular flows and the mechanisms of particle-size segregation, alongside shape-dependent flow and dynamic column collapse.

04

Coupled simulation for civil engineering

Developing DEM–FEM models for reinforced concrete cracking, DEM–FVM simulations for underwater bentonite pellet filling, and DEM–SPH models for fresh concrete flow and clogging. DEM modeling also supports excavation face stability assessment in shield tunneling.
